## Epics Detail

### EP-001: Account Opening & Onboarding

**Business Goal:** Enable customers to open a current account digitally; reduce account opening turnaround time; increase customer acquisition and retention.

**Scope:**
- In scope: Current account application, eligibility validation, account creation, debit card issuance
- Out of scope: Loan processing, credit card applications, business current accounts

**Stakeholders:** Customer, Relationship Manager, Operations Team, Branch Staff, IT Team

#### Features

| Feature ID  | Feature Name               | Description                                                                 |
|-------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| EP-001-F01  | Account Application        | Customer initiates and submits a current account application                |
| EP-001-F02  | Eligibility Validation     | System validates customer eligibility (age, ID, blacklist check)            |
| EP-001-F03  | Account Creation           | System creates the account and generates a unique account number            |
| EP-001-F04  | Debit Card Request         | Customer can opt-in to receive a debit card during account opening          |

#### BDD Scenarios (High Level)

```gherkin
Feature: Account Application

  Background:
    Given the customer is on the account opening page

  Scenario: Successful account application submission
    Given the customer has filled in all mandatory fields
    When  the customer submits the application
    Then  the application is accepted for processing
    And   the customer receives an acknowledgement notification

  Scenario: Application rejected due to missing mandatory fields
    Given the customer has left mandatory fields empty
    When  the customer attempts to submit the application
    Then  the system displays validation errors for the missing fields
    And   the application is not submitted

Feature: Eligibility Validation

  Background:
    Given a customer has submitted an account application

  Scenario: Customer meets all eligibility criteria
    Given the customer is at least 18 years old
    And   the customer possesses valid identification
    And   the customer is not blacklisted
    When  the system performs eligibility validation
    Then  the application proceeds to the next step

  Scenario: Customer is under 18 years old
    Given the customer is under 18 years old
    When  the system performs eligibility validation
    Then  the application is rejected
    And   the customer is informed of the age requirement

  Scenario: Customer is blacklisted
    Given the customer exists on the blacklist
    When  the system performs eligibility validation
    Then  the application is rejected
    And   the customer is informed they cannot open an account

Feature: Account Creation

  Background:
    Given a customer application has passed all verifications

  Scenario: Successful account creation
    When  the system creates the current account
    Then  a unique account number is generated
    And   the account status is marked as Active
    And   the customer receives a confirmation notification

Feature: Debit Card Request

  Background:
    Given a customer is completing account opening

  Scenario: Customer opts in for a debit card
    Given the customer selects the debit card option
    When  the account is successfully created
    Then  a card request is sent to the card management system
    And   the customer receives card tracking information

  Scenario: Customer opts out of debit card
    Given the customer declines the debit card option
    When  the account is successfully created
    Then  no card request is generated
```

#### Constraints (from NFRs)
- System availability of 99.9% (NFR-001)
- Response time under 3 seconds (NFR-002)
- Data encrypted in transit and at rest (NFR-003)
- Mobile responsive user interface (NFR-006)

### EP-002: Identity Verification (KYC)

**Business Goal:** Ensure compliance with banking regulations and KYC requirements; protect the bank from fraud and regulatory risk.

**Scope:**
- In scope: Customer identity verification, KYC document upload and validation
- Out of scope: Investment products, business current accounts

**Stakeholders:** Customer, Compliance Team, Operations Team, IT Team

#### Features

| Feature ID  | Feature Name                | Description                                                              |
|-------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| EP-002-F01  | KYC Document Upload         | Customer uploads identity documents for verification                     |
| EP-002-F02  | Identity Verification       | System verifies customer identity against submitted documents            |
| EP-002-F03  | KYC Status Tracking         | Customer and staff can view verification status                          |

#### BDD Scenarios (High Level)

```gherkin
Feature: KYC Document Upload

  Background:
    Given the customer is at the KYC verification step

  Scenario Outline: Customer uploads a valid KYC document
    Given the customer has a valid <document_type>
    When  the customer uploads the <document_type>
    Then  the document is accepted for verification

    Examples:
      | document_type   |
      | Aadhaar         |
      | PAN             |
      | Passport        |
      | Driving License |

  Scenario: Customer uploads an unsupported document type
    Given the customer has a document not in the approved list
    When  the customer attempts to upload the document
    Then  the system rejects the upload
    And   displays a message listing accepted document types

Feature: Identity Verification

  Background:
    Given the customer has uploaded KYC documents

  Scenario: KYC verification succeeds
    When  the system verifies the customer identity
    Then  the KYC status is updated to Verified
    And   the application proceeds to account creation

  Scenario: KYC verification fails
    When  the system cannot verify the customer identity
    Then  the KYC status is updated to Failed
    And   the application cannot proceed
    And   the customer is notified of the failure

Feature: KYC Status Tracking

  Scenario: Customer views KYC verification status
    Given the customer has submitted KYC documents
    When  the customer checks their application status
    Then  the current KYC verification status is displayed
```

#### Constraints (from NFRs)
- Regulatory compliance maintained (NFR-004)
- Audit logging enabled (NFR-005)
- Data encrypted in transit and at rest (NFR-003)

### EP-003: Digital Banking Enrollment

**Business Goal:** Provide seamless banking services through online and mobile channels; reduce branch visits by 40%.

**Scope:**
- In scope: Online banking enrollment, mobile banking enrollment
- Out of scope: Loan processing, credit card applications, investment products

**Stakeholders:** Customer, IT Team, Relationship Manager

#### Features

| Feature ID  | Feature Name                  | Description                                                          |
|-------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| EP-003-F01  | Online Banking Registration   | Customer registers for internet banking with secure credentials      |
| EP-003-F02  | Multi-Factor Authentication   | MFA enabled during online banking registration                       |

#### BDD Scenarios (High Level)

```gherkin
Feature: Online Banking Registration

  Background:
    Given the customer has an active current account

  Scenario: Successful online banking registration
    Given the customer initiates online banking registration
    When  the system generates user credentials securely
    And   multi-factor authentication is enabled
    Then  the customer receives a registration confirmation
    And   the customer can access online banking

  Scenario: Registration fails due to system error
    Given the customer initiates online banking registration
    When  credential generation fails
    Then  the customer is informed of the failure
    And   the customer is advised to retry or contact support

Feature: Multi-Factor Authentication

  Scenario: MFA setup during registration
    Given the customer is completing online banking registration
    When  the customer is prompted to set up MFA
    Then  MFA options are presented (SMS, authenticator app)
    And   MFA is activated upon successful setup
```

⚠️ OPEN QUESTION: The BRD mentions both "Online banking enrollment" and "Mobile banking enrollment" as separate in-scope items but BR-006 only covers internet banking registration. Is mobile banking enrollment a separate registration flow or is it included as part of online banking registration?

#### Constraints (from NFRs)
- System availability of 99.9% (NFR-001)
- Data encrypted in transit and at rest (NFR-003)
- Mobile responsive user interface (NFR-006)

### EP-004: Transaction & Account Management

**Business Goal:** Support daily banking transactions; improve customer experience; achieve customer satisfaction score above 4.5/5.

**Scope:**
- In scope: Transaction history viewing, funds transfer, balance inquiry, statement generation, account maintenance
- Out of scope: Loan processing, credit card applications, investment products

**Stakeholders:** Customer, Relationship Manager, Operations Team, IT Team

#### Features

| Feature ID  | Feature Name             | Description                                                           |
|-------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| EP-004-F01  | Funds Transfer           | Customer can transfer funds from their current account                |
| EP-004-F02  | Balance Inquiry          | Customer can check their account balance                              |
| EP-004-F03  | Transaction History      | Customer can view past transactions                                   |
| EP-004-F04  | Statement Generation     | Customer can generate and download account statements                 |

#### BDD Scenarios (High Level)

```gherkin
Feature: Funds Transfer

  Background:
    Given the customer has an active current account
    And   the customer is logged in to digital banking

  Scenario: Successful funds transfer
    Given the customer has sufficient balance
    When  the customer initiates a funds transfer with valid details
    Then  the transfer is processed successfully
    And   the transaction appears in the transaction history

  Scenario: Funds transfer with insufficient balance
    Given the customer does not have sufficient balance
    When  the customer initiates a funds transfer
    Then  the transfer is declined
    And   the customer is notified of insufficient funds

Feature: Balance Inquiry

  Scenario: Customer checks account balance
    Given the customer has an active current account
    And   the customer is logged in to digital banking
    When  the customer requests a balance inquiry
    Then  the current account balance is displayed

Feature: Transaction History

  Background:
    Given the customer has an active current account
    And   the customer is logged in to digital banking

  Scenario: Customer views transaction history
    When  the customer navigates to transaction history
    Then  past transactions are displayed with date, description, and amount

Feature: Statement Generation

  Background:
    Given the customer has an active current account
    And   the customer is logged in to digital banking

  Scenario: Customer generates a statement for a date range
    Given the customer selects a valid date range
    When  the customer requests a statement
    Then  a statement in PDF format is generated
    And   the statement is available for download and printing

  Scenario: Customer selects an invalid date range
    Given the customer selects a date range with start date after end date
    When  the customer requests a statement
    Then  the system displays a validation error
```

#### Constraints (from NFRs)
- Response time under 3 seconds (NFR-002)
- Audit logging enabled (NFR-005)
- System availability of 99.9% (NFR-001)
